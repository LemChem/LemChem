About LemChem

LemChem is the digital home of interactive chemistry education projects. Created by Jessy M. Lemieux, Ph.D., a chemist and community college instructor, LemChem is built on a simple mission: to make the wonders of the physical world accessible, engaging, and genuinely enjoyable to learn for everyone.

The flagship project, Ages of Chemistry, invites players to combine base elements and trace the entire arc of human chemical discovery—from primordial water and stone to engineering modern semiconductors.

Science is fun! It's about time learning science was fun too.
