Ages of Chemistry

Version 0.8.9

LemChem is the home of interactive chemistry education projects created by Jessy M. Lemieux, Ph.D., a chemist and college instructor on a mission to make science genuinely fun to learn. Ages of Chemistry is the flagship project, with more games and tools on the way.

LemChem is a passion project offered for free. Your donations help cover hosting costs and will fund specialists to make every project even better.
